01 / Who I help

Companies are about to build more than ever. Most won't know what's worth building.

With AI, companies can now build their own tools quickly and cheaply. Many will fail the way most startups fail: by building something nobody needed. They start from a tool or an idea, not from a real problem that costs money. Pilots drag on, never adopted and never stopped.

02 / How it works

  1. Find who has the problem. Talk to the people who feel it every day, your staff or your customers. Find out what it costs now: money, hours, or a number you already track.
  2. Set the test. Agree on one number the pilot must move, how much counts as a pass, and a deadline.
  3. Build a working demo. On your real data, used by the real people, in weeks, not months.
  4. Decide. If the number moved, scale it. If not, change it or stop it.
  5. Hand it over. What works goes to the team who'll run it every day.
